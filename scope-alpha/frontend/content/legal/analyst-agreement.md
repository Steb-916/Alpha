Signals are timestamped at the moment of publication and become part of your public performance record. **You may not edit the price levels, direction, asset, or timing of a published signal.** You may only update notes after publication, and you may cancel an active signal that has not yet hit a target.

## 2. No Manipulative Practices

You agree not to engage in any of the following:

- **Front-running**: trading ahead of signals you publish or know are about to be published
- **Wash trading**: simulating activity to influence the apparent demand or price of an asset
- **Pump and dump schemes**: publishing signals on illiquid assets to manipulate price for personal gain
- **Coordinated manipulation**: working with other analysts to create false impressions of consensus
